# Fictional staff register

These original team-created text documents simulate staff-entered data when bills
or other corroborating records are unavailable. All names, quantities and events
are invented. They are not real company records, verified disclosures or training
examples for an authenticity detector.

The register deliberately includes unsupported Social/Governance requirements to
show manual handling: having a staff entry does not expand the mapper's supported
SEDG scope. Missing evidence remains missing. Review acceptance confirms only the
recorded decision; it does not turn an unsupported assertion into verified data.

Evidence strength: low. Review urgency: normally P2, raised to P1 for conflicts or
material exceptions. Low strength never means low urgency automatically.

The original four receipt-category folders and their frozen manifest are unchanged.
This supplemental fixture is separate from the 360-image OCR comparison dataset
and is not part of its model scores or train/validation splits.
